Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common cloud migration, cost optimization, and CI/CD automation queries.

How does DevOps automation reduce our business operating costs?
Automated CI/CD pipelines eliminate hours of manual server configuration, prevent costly human deployment errors, enable automatic server downscaling during quiet hours, and identify over-provisioned cloud instances, frequently cutting monthly hosting bills by 30% to 45%.
Can you migrate our application to GCP or AWS without downtime?
Yes! We build the new containerized cloud environment in parallel, replicate databases in real-time, test under load, and perform a seamless DNS cutover via Cloudflare so your end users experience zero service interruption.
When should our company adopt Kubernetes (K8s)?
If your application consists of multiple microservices with unpredictable traffic spikes, requires automatic scaling across multiple container replicas, or needs zero-downtime rolling updates, Kubernetes (GKE or EKS) is the gold standard for orchestration.
How do you guarantee our cloud infrastructure is secure?
We enforce strict least-privilege IAM policies, isolate databases in private subnets with NAT gateways, scan Docker images for CVE vulnerabilities before deployment, and deploy Cloudflare WAF to stop bot attacks and DDoS floods.
