Our Heritage & Craft

Baking Memories Since 1998

What started as a small family kitchen has grown into the heart of the neighborhood. At The Golden Crumb, we believe that great bread takes time, patience, and the finest ingredients.

We source local, organic flour and mill it in-house. No preservatives, no shortcuts—just pure, delicious baking passed down through three generations.
