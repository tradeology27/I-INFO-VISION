FOUR PILLARS OF DESSAU

The core axioms formulated by Walter Gropius and Johannes Itten.

// 1919-2026
01

Form Follows Function

The appearance of any UI element must stem directly from its purpose. Every layout decision solves a human interaction problem.

02

Asymmetrical Dynamic Balance

Rejecting rigid classical symmetry in favor of energetic offset axes that guide visual hierarchy with natural kinetic tension.

03

Primary Color Purity

Red, yellow, and blue create universal clarity without muted pastel deception. Instant cognitive recognition across cultures.

04

Honesty of Materials

Steel, glass, concrete, and unvarnished code. Digital components proudly display their structural borders and semantic roles.
