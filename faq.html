Custom Software & Enterprise ERP

Custom ERP How is a custom ERP better than off-the-shelf software like SAP, Tally, or Zoho?

While packaged software works for generic accounting, growing businesses in specialized sectors (such as textile mills, sago production, fabrication, and multi-branch retail) quickly encounter roadblocks:

  • No Per-User Subscription Tax: Commercial SaaS charges monthly fees per user. As your staff grows from 10 to 100 users, annual software costs explode. Our custom ERPs come with unlimited user accounts on your private server.
  • Matches Your Exact Shop-Floor Process: You do not need to alter your factory or warehouse operations to fit rigid software constraints. We tailor the database fields, approval chains, and reports to your exact operational procedures.
  • Real-Time Hardware & WhatsApp Integration: Seamlessly connect weighing scales, thermal barcode printers, RFID scanners, and automated WhatsApp alert bots for payment receipts and dispatch confirmations.

GST Compliance Does your billing and POS software support automated GST e-Invoicing & e-Way Bills?

Yes. All our billing software and custom ERP solutions are 100% compliant with Indian GST requirements.

Features include:

  • Direct Government e-Invoicing Portal API integration (generating IRN numbers and signed QR codes directly on invoices in 1.2 seconds).
  • Automated Part-A and Part-B e-Way Bill generation directly from dispatch slips.
  • One-click GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and GSTR-9 tax reconciliation summaries in JSON and Excel formats ready for chartered accountants.

Legacy Migration Can you migrate data from our existing Tally, Excel sheets, or legacy databases?

Yes. We conduct automated ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) migrations from Tally XML, SQL Server, MySQL, Access, and multi-year Excel spreadsheets.

Our data engineers clean duplicate customer accounts, sanitize inventory SKUs, map opening balances, and verify historical financial ledgers with zero downtime during the cutover weekend.

Mobile Access Can our managers and field sales teams access the software on mobile phones?

Yes. Every system we engineer features a responsive, mobile-optimized progressive web app (PWA) or native Android/iOS companion application.

Executives can monitor live sales dashboards, approve purchase orders, track field agent geo-locations, and generate outstanding statements from any smartphone anywhere in the world.

Source Code, Security & IP Ownership

IP Ownership Do we own the full source code and database rights?

Yes, 100%. You hold unrestricted legal ownership of your entire software asset.

Upon project sign-off and final payment settlement, we push all Git repositories, compiled production bundles, schema migration files, API documentation, and server configuration scripts directly to your private accounts. You are never locked in or forced into continuing subscriptions.

Confidentiality Do you sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before discussing proprietary business ideas?

Yes. We routinely execute mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to examining your proprietary workflows, algorithms, or client databases.

All engineers and architects on our staff are bound by strict non-disclosure obligations, and all internal source code repositories are isolated with multi-factor authentication and role-based access control.

Data Backups How do you ensure our production business data is safe from crashes or ransomware?

We implement military-grade backup and recovery protocols:

  • Automated 6-hour point-in-time database snapshots stored across geographically redundant S3/Cloud storage buckets.
  • End-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Automated health check monitors alerting our DevOps team immediately in the event of unexpected memory spikes or connection anomalies.
