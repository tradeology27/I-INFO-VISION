Project Overview

AllenSha.com is a modern, high-performance website developed by I Info Vision for Dr. M. Mohamed Shahid, BHMS, MD(Hom) – a Gold Medalist and Government Registered Medical Practitioner (Reg. No: 3459) specializing in classical homeopathy. The clinic, established since 2010 in Salem, Tamil Nadu, offers 100% online consultations via video/phone from 3:00 PM to 9:00 PM with doorstep medicine delivery across India.

The website was designed to reflect the clinic's holistic approach to healthcare while maximizing patient conversions through intuitive UX, fast load times, and prominent call-to-action elements. We implemented a complete digital presence strategy including SEO optimization, Google Business Profile integration, and WhatsApp-based patient engagement.

The site features a clean, nature-inspired design with earthy tones of green and cream, matching the homeopathy clinic's brand identity. Key sections include treatment listings for hair loss, skin conditions, allergies, and chronic conditions, a doctor profile with credentials, patient reviews section showcasing 860+ Google reviews with a 4.9 rating, and seamless booking integration.
