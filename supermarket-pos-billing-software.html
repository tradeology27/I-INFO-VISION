Frequently Asked Questions

Can it handle continuous billing when the internet is disconnected?

Yes, full offline-first functionality ensures checkout counters never stop billing even during total internet blackouts.

Can we print barcode price stickers for loose pulses and grains?

Yes, print custom barcode stickers with item name, packed date, weight, and price directly from the software.

Does it prevent staff theft and cash drawer mismatches?

Yes, cashier shift closing requires blind cash tallying, and voids/deletions require manager password authorization.

Can customers pay with split payments (Cash + UPI)?

Yes, cashiers can split single bills across multiple tender modes including Google Pay, PhonePe, cash, and sodexo cards.
