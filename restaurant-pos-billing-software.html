Frequently Asked Questions

Can it print KOTs to different kitchen sections (Tandoor, Chinese, Bar)?

Yes, orders automatically split and print to designated kitchen stations (e.g., beverages to bar, gravies to main kitchen).

Can customers pay using dynamic UPI QR codes printed on the bill?

Yes, dynamic UPI QR codes print directly on the guest bill so customers scan and pay in 5 seconds.

How does recipe management prevent kitchen ingredient theft?

By mapping raw ingredients to each dish, the software shows variance between expected ingredient usage and physical stock left.

Can it handle AC, Non-AC, and Parcel different price lists?

Yes, configure separate pricing tiers for AC dine-in, non-AC dining, room service, and parcel take-away.
