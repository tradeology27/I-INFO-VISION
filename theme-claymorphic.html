Theme DNA & Architecture

Claymorphic Soft 3D

Concept & Vibe

An evolution of neumorphism into soft 3D marshmallow tactile realism. High-radius rounded geometry paired with dual inner bevels and diffused drop-shadows create interactive depth that invites user touch.
