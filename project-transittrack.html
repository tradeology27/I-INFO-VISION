Project Overview

TransitTrack is a modern logistics dispatch and fleet telemetry application engineered by I Info Vision. Designed for transport fleet operators, shipping carriers, and intercity logistics providers, it provides complete operational visibility from warehouse loading to final delivery.

Built with cross-platform Flutter and backed by Google Maps Platform APIs, Firebase, and Supabase, TransitTrack delivers real-time truck geolocations, turn-by-turn route adherence monitoring, and instant driver trip dispatching.

The platform includes IoT telemetry processing that tracks fuel consumption rates, vehicle idling duration, speed violations, and automated geofence entry/exit alerts, significantly reducing transit overheads and fuel wastage.

Key Engineering Deliverables

Real-Time GPS Tracking

Sub-second GPS vehicle position polling on Google Maps with live speed, direction, and route trail.

Dynamic Route Optimizer

Smart multi-stop waypoint sequencing that cuts driving mileage and bypasses congested traffic corridors.

Fuel Telematics & Mileage

Liters-per-100km telemetry dashboards, fuel siphon alerts, and driver idling behavior scoring.

Driver Mobile App

Dedicated Flutter mobile app for drivers with trip manifestation, digital ePOD signatures, and toll tracking.

Geofencing & Depot Alerts

Automated warehouse arrival and departure timestamps eliminating loading bay wait-time disputes.

Trip Billing & e-Way Bill

Trip sheet generation, GST consignment notes, driver allowance calculation, and freight invoicing.
