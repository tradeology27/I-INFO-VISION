Frequently Asked Questions

Does it calculate the exact net profit made on every trip?

Yes, calculates freight revenue minus diesel, driver beta, toll, loading charges, and maintenance to give exact net trip margin.

Can it integrate with our existing vehicle GPS trackers?

Yes, we integrate with all major GPS tracker protocols (Concox, Teltonika, Sinotrack) via secure telemetry APIs.

How does tyre tracking save money for fleet owners?

By tracking mileage and timely wheel rotation, tyre life increases by up to 25%, saving lakhs in replacement costs.

Can drivers submit diesel receipts and expense photos on mobile?

Yes, drivers can snap fuel bill photos and submit expenses through the driver mobile app for instant manager verification.
