Kumar Hospital Billing Software

Custom Healthcare Billing & Patient Management System for KNCH

Project Overview

I Info Vision developed a comprehensive, custom billing software for Kumar Nature Cure Hospital & Dental in Karur, Tamil Nadu. The hospital required a robust billing system tailored to their unique requirements as a naturopathy and dental care center – handling diverse treatment packages, therapy sessions, dental procedures, and medication-free consultation billing.

The software was designed from the ground up to streamline the hospital's entire billing workflow – from patient registration and appointment booking to treatment invoicing and payment reconciliation. The system supports GST-compliant billing with automatic tax calculations, multiple payment modes (cash, card, UPI, online), and generates detailed financial reports for accounting and compliance.

Key challenges addressed included creating a flexible billing structure for nature cure packages (which differ from conventional hospital billing), integrating dental treatment costing, managing patient follow-up schedules, and generating treatment-wise revenue analytics. The software runs on a local network at the hospital's reception with role-based access for receptionists, doctors, and administrators.

Software Features

GST-Compliant Invoicing

Automated GST calculation with CGST/SGST/IGST breakdown, HSN/SAC codes, and compliant invoice generation for all treatments.

Patient Records Management

Complete patient database with medical history, treatment records, appointment history, and quick search functionality.

Appointment Scheduling

Doctor-wise appointment management with slot booking, reminders, and real-time availability tracking for therapy sessions.

Multi-Payment Support

Accept and track payments via cash, card, UPI, and online transfers with automated payment reconciliation.

Revenue Analytics

Treatment-wise, doctor-wise, and period-wise revenue reports with visual dashboards for management insights.

Role-Based Access Control

Secure multi-user system with separate access levels for receptionist, doctors, billing staff, and administrators.

Thermal Print Support

Direct thermal printer integration for instant bill printing at the reception counter with customizable receipt formats.

Automated Backups

Daily automatic database backups with cloud sync option to prevent data loss and ensure business continuity.
